# Dataset approval note — CA4 Project 37

**Proposed title:** Hotel Revenue & Occupancy Analytics

**Problem statement:** A hotel chain wants to understand booking demand, average daily rates, booking channels, cancellations and customer segments to identify revenue opportunities and operational patterns.

**Proposed dataset:** Hotel Booking Demand dataset.

**Public dataset link:** https://github.com/aaqibqadeer/Hotel-booking-demand/blob/master/hotel_bookings.csv

**Original research/source article:** Antonio, N., de Almeida, A., & Nunes, L. (2019). *Hotel booking demand datasets*. Data in Brief, 22, 41–49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126

**Why it is relevant:** The dataset contains booking-level records for a city hotel and a resort hotel, including booking status, lead time, arrival date components, stay duration, ADR, market segment, distribution channel and customer type. The original article describes 119,390 observations in total across the two hotel datasets.

**Proposed analysis:** Data quality checks, missing-value and duplicate review, booking trends, ADR comparisons, cancellation analysis, channel and customer segment analysis, interactive Streamlit dashboard, evidence-based recommendations.

**Important limitation:** Total room inventory and daily room availability are not provided, so a true occupancy percentage cannot be calculated. The project will clearly report booking demand indicators and an ADR-times-nights room-value proxy instead of claiming audited revenue or true occupancy.

**Approval requested:** Please approve the project title and dataset before analysis begins, as required by the CA4 instructions.
